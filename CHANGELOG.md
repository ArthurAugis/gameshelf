# Changelog

Each release is listed here and in the app (click the version number in the header).

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-06

- Import a whole folder of ROMs or PC games at once: one entry per game, with the console guessed and the cover searched by name

## 0.2.1 - 2026-10-05

- Automatic update: at launch GameShelf offers a new release, installs it and starts again
- The version number in the header opens this changelog
- Long titles shrink to fit the top and back of the 3D box

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-03

- Add games by hand: any program or emulator game, with cover search (Steam and libretro), console logos, editing, shortcut import and emulator presets
- GOG Galaxy updates appear in the Updates panel
- The star rating can be cleared again, with a Clear link or a right click
- The window, header, game page, filters and dialogs adapt to small windows

## 0.1.2 - 2026-10-03

- GOG Galaxy library: installed and owned games, Play and Uninstall

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-03

- Epic Games: uninstall and cancel a download without the launcher dialog
- Epic downloads start minimized, and the launcher closes when it is idle
- Restart Epic works when the launcher is closed or only in the tray

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-02

- First release: your Steam and Epic Games library as a virtual bookshelf
- Game page with a 3D box, notes, ratings and statuses, collections
- Search, filters, sorting and grouping, skins, controller and fullscreen mode
- English, French, Spanish and German
